Original models and other artwork authored for the AE2 Machines concept.

and spatial_storage_cell_128.png from Applied Energistics 2 19.2.17 (Minecraft 1.21.1).

They are copied under ae2machines names so both supported versions use the requested
1.21.1 artwork without altering the native AE2 resources.

The virtual spatial IO port also uses the AE2 19.2.17 spatial_io_port and
spatial_io_port_on models, four front/top state textures and the generic
back/bottom/side textures. Only resource namespaces are adapted; geometry,
UVs, transforms and pixels are unchanged. The same AE2 artwork credits and
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license above apply to these imported assets.
